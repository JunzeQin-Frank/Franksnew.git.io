project

The goal of this trash can is to automatically dump the trash that been placed on the top of the plate. After the user press the red bottom, the arms will follow a pre-determined trace to dump the trash into the container with out leaking. The trace is simulated using MID to refine the dimensions of the arms and their locations.

Experience

Researchers at MIT have synthesized an ultrastrong two-dimensional polymeric material called 2DPA-1, which consists of hydrogen-bonded 2D polymer platelets with film thicknesses ranging from 3 to 60 nm. They have found that its yield strength is approximately 490 MPa—twice that of steel—while its density is only about one-sixth that of steel.

Our lab’s goal is to study the mechanical properties of the film and explore potential methods for preventing gas leakage by reducing the gaps within the material. Currently, I have pressurized the film with various gases, including nitrogen, argon, and oxygen, and have begun experimenting with a UV cleaner. While we are aware that the UV cleaner creates tiny pinholes in the film, our next step is to identify methods for reversing this process and filling the gaps in the membrane.

Since the summer of 2024, I have also started working at BU's largest machine shop, EPIC. As a student employee, my role involves processing and assembling orders for both clients and students using milling CNC machines, laser cutters, and wire EDM machines. Typically, our team assesses customers' orders and divides the components into different positions to ensure overall efficiency. My responsibilities also include using CAD software and 3D printers to design and manufacture parts as per students' specifications.
